## 12. Outside the app

What surrounds the product, as the user meets it.

- **User documentation** (French, for the public), **online at `help.<domain>`** —
  maintained outside this repository. Its accuracy is a trust obligation (root rule 8): a
  change to what redaction protects, to a data flow or to what a surface can do says so in
  the change, so the site follows.
- **A hosted stack**, optional (accounts, billing, sync, organizations, included models,
  server-side redaction): the app is its client only. Which of it EXISTS in a build is
  decided by the build's variables (§ 1 of the index); without them the app runs whole on
  your own keys and local models.
