French social security numbers (NIR) are recognised in more written forms: dash-separated groups, the 13 digits without their key behind a keyword (« N° SS », « NIR », « sécu »), special months with a valid key, and an OCR O-for-zero behind a keyword. A « N° SIRET » label above its number is no longer read as a company name, and the last group of a SIRET is no longer read as a postal code.
